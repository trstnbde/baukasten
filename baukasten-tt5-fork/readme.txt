A standalone fork of Twenty Twenty-Five 1.5 with three design systems as styles: Organic (the default), Modernist and Nocturne.

== Description ==

Baukasten Theme: TT5 Fork keeps everything that makes Twenty Twenty-Five useful — its templates, template parts, patterns, section styles and block styles — and replaces its look with three design systems, each a complete style variation:

* **Organic** (the default) — warm sand and terracotta with a sage second accent, Caprasimo headings over Figtree, generous rounded frames, pill-shaped buttons and softly washed-out photographs.
* **Modernist** — flat and architectural, set entirely in Archivo: near-mono red on off-white, no rounded corners, strong 2px rules, flush-left button labels and black-and-white photographs.
* **Nocturne** — a quiet, compact dark interface: blue-grey ground, Inter at medium weight, 8px radii, outlined buttons, rules that fade out at both ends and photographs that blend into the page.

Two font presets, **Noto Sans** and **Noto Serif**, replace the body and heading faces in any of the three.

The theme is standalone: Twenty Twenty-Five does not need to be installed. All fonts are bundled, so the theme sends no requests to third parties. Twenty Twenty-Five's own eight style variations and its color and font presets are not included.

Choose a style under Appearance → Editor → Styles.
